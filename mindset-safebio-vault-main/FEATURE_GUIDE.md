# 🧠 MindSet X SafeBio Vault - Complete Feature Guide

## 🎯 Overview

MindSet X SafeBio Vault is a comprehensive mental health & biological data management platform powered by Google Gemini AI. It combines mental health support, environmental health analysis, biometric tracking, and privacy-preserving medical data management.

## 🚀 CORE FEATURES

### 1. 🤖 **MindSet AI Chat (Triage & Support)**

**Access**: Main screen → Chat/MindSet tab

**What it does**:
- Real-time mental health consultation with AI
- Sentiment analysis for mood tracking
- PHQ-9 mental health assessment questionnaire
- Crisis detection and emergency support recommendations
- Multi-language support (English & Hinglish)
- Reference sources for advice

**How to use**:
1. Open the Chat Interface
2. Type your mental health concerns
3. AI responds with empathetic support
4. Ask for "Start Assessment" to take PHQ-9 test
5. Your sentiment scores are tracked in Sentinel Dashboard

**Example queries**:
- "I'm feeling very stressed about exams"
- "I can't sleep at night"
- "Start Assessment"

### 4. 🌍 **SDoH Diagnostic Engine (Environmental Health)**

**Access**: SafeBio Vault tab → SDoH Engine

**What it does**:
- Analyzes your LOCAL ENVIRONMENT (pollution, weather, diseases)
- Combines with your SYMPTOMS
- Calculates holistic health risk score (0-100)
- Provides environment-specific prescriptions

**Environmental Factors Analyzed**:
- 🌫️ Air Quality Index (AQI)
- 🌡️ Temperature & Humidity
- 🦟 Disease Outbreaks (Dengue, Malaria, Flu)
- 📍 Location-specific health risks

**How to use**:
1. Open SafeBio Vault → SDoH Engine
2. Click "Enable Location" to share your coordinates
3. Describe your symptoms in the chat
4. Optionally upload a blood report or medical prescription
5. AI provides:
   - Environmental Context (AQI, weather, outbreaks)
   - Clinical Analysis (symptom severity)
   - Risk Score (0-100)
   - SDoH Prescription (actionable advice)

**Example**:
- **Input**: "I have cough and shortness of breath" (in Delhi)
- **Output**: "High AQI detected (234). Avoid outdoor walks. Use air purifier. Wear N95 mask."
